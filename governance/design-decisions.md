# Design Decisions

The reasons behind the kit's file-level and behavioural choices. The constitution owns operating rules, ADRs own structural decisions, and product baselines own current deployment state. This register preserves rationale and rejected alternatives without becoming another copy of those sources.

## Authority and loading are explicit

Identity and thinking style change slowly; products change frequently. Plain Markdown keeps the constitution portable while product syntax and configuration stay in implementation. Portability of format does not establish equivalent behaviour.

Files may arrive out of order, so each constitution file declares its classification and the bootstrap supplies the complete hierarchy. Calibration shapes interpretation, binding rules control behaviour, and the professional overlay supplements them when relevant. Numbered filenames aid navigation but cannot replace those declarations. Internal authority remains subject to host controls; reviewing proposed instructions or evidence does not activate them. Ordinary work needs no recital of the hierarchy.

The **full-core route** supplies bootstrap, calibration and operating contract together. The professional overlay is conditional: Microsoft/CDTO work, professional UK government engagement or explicit invocation. Kit maintenance keeps it dormant unless explicitly invoked for relevant work. The supported condensed routes supply a standalone derivation instead of the full core; this is not an exception to file precedence. The bootstrap and [loading combinations](../kit/implementation/platforms/contract-maintenance.md#loading-combinations) own the exact rules.

## Independent judgement determines mode and contribution

The kit should extend Andrew's thinking without doing it for him. Plausible, fluent analysis can become a received position before he has tested it; familiar voice can make that easier to miss. Calibration records personal thinking moves to test and extend, rather than theses to prime unrelated analysis. Protecting judgement leaves both current beliefs and habitual reasoning methods open to revision. [ADR-007](decisions/adr-007-evolution-objective.md) records the paired protection and evolution objective.

Exploration therefore brings applied lenses and unfinished alternatives while leaving the worked conclusion with Andrew. Execution produces the requested deliverable. The same developed argument can be useful in execution and premature in exploration; a product label cannot decide between them. Explicit “withhold” and “produce” posture overrides make costly misreads correctable.

Plural, disposable sketches are exploratory contribution. Reaction can reveal a preference Andrew could not specify, but selecting a sketch does not commission the finished work. [C4/C5](../kit/evals/provoke-produce-probes.md) records that distinction. An optional personal practice is to frame a problem independently before reading the assistant's developed view. The contract's light invitation preserves that opportunity without turning a defined analysis into a compulsory exercise. Neither the practice nor fluent output proves that independence is protected.

The [judgement adaptation amendment](evidence/2026-09-judgement-adaptation.md) makes three responsibilities explicit: challenge a familiar thinking method when it obscures consequential evidence; identify diminishing value in further work; and examine consequential emotional or intuitive signals without treating their explanations as established facts. A value conflict may remain after the facts are known. These changes refine the existing evolution objective and quality bar; they add no role or authority layer.

Personality labels and scores were excluded from standing instructions. The amendment defines observable situations and responses rather than assuming a trait or defect. Sufficiency advice preserves agreed standards, required checks and the choice to keep exploring for learning. Method challenge must retain approaches that fit. The [paired probes](../kit/evals/judgement-adaptation-probes.md) test useful intervention alongside restraint; a plausible rule or a source check does not establish improved behaviour.

## Professional context separates enduring guidance from changing facts

The [professional-context refresh](evidence/2026-09-professional-context-refresh.md) follows an interview confirming Andrew's role, commercial accountability, work across customer and Microsoft organisations, and use of AI for research, learning and proposition development. Calibration holds the fixed career anchor and experimentation across providers. The conditional overlay holds professional application, including organisational change, skills, internal coordination and customer decision context.

Professional lenses are an open starting set. A prescribed count and mandatory tour through every perspective were rejected because relevance depends on the problem. The customer's constraints and Microsoft's ability to organise support both matter; commercial accountability remains visible without predetermining the customer's best outcome. Broadly adopted AI narratives remain open to challenge, with firsthand experience, published research and extrapolation distinguished.

The account alignment and SPA24 are retained as dated context because they help explain the working remit. Named colleagues, temporary initiatives, publication catalogues, headcount and rolling tenure phrases are omitted. Historical start dates retain meaning as time passes; current responsibilities and agreement status need checking when material. A new file version is not a factual revalidation. Moving the entire overlay into memory was unnecessary: its durable professional guidance remains constitutional, while changing task material stays outside the standing instructions.

The CDTO identity and activation boundary are unchanged. Condensed contracts deliberately omit the detailed professional context; their existing evidence and ownership rules remain applicable. No new role, authority layer or structural ADR is introduced.

## Execution preserves authority, substance and evidence

A defined task should be completed using its established decisions and rationale. Clarification depends on material ambiguity, not task size: ask when a missing fact, target or decision changes the result; proceed when the brief already supplies it. Refinement preserves meaning, stance and ownership. Visible structure, rationale, evidence and trade-offs make the work reviewable without asking for private model reasoning.

A specified change request or approval to implement a defined plan authorises its scoped batch. It does not settle open choices, approve substitutes or waive narrower safeguards. Assessment does not approve fixes, and a draft does not approve sending. This boundary covers persistent instructions and hidden supporting records as well as visible deliverables; access alone is not authority to adapt the contract.

Tool-mediated progress and completion need current-session evidence for the exact approved target. A successful command is insufficient when the requested result remains unverified. Missing targets, failed checks and uncertainty are outcomes to report, not reasons to substitute a plausible target or round up to completion. The [operating contract](../kit/constitution/02-operating-contract.md) owns these rules across surfaces.

## Voice craft lives in a skill; durable principles stay portable

The assistant reasons in a neutral partner register. Andrew's personal voice belongs to requested output whose substance he supplied or adopted. The separation protects scrutiny: a generic argument wearing familiar voice is easier to accept as one's own. The partner standard is self-contained in the constitution and does not need the execution-only skill for sentence-level hygiene.

The constitution owns voice separation, ownership, audience, purpose-sensitive structure and durable writing principles. `my-voice` names the capability plainly and adds routing, craft and examples. Moving all voice policy into the skill was rejected because the boundaries must remain available on surfaces that cannot load it. Model and host advice stays outside the skill.

The four-file package carries a bounded derivation of audience and form guidance for a condensed operating base. Its register references resolve locally; constitution paths identify provenance rather than requiring an unavailable repository. The full core governs when supplied. Per-client craft forks were rejected because they would create competing owners. The [Agent Plugins wrapper](../kit/implementation/plugins/my-voice/README.md) derives a distribution copy, omitting one client-specific metadata field while preserving the body and references.

[ADR-013](decisions/adr-013-generated-plugin-marketplace.md) adds a generated marketplace release branch, a shared catalogue and generated package versions. Version identity supports host refresh; it does not introduce document version markers into the skill. The [marketplace record](evidence/2026-09-voice-marketplace.md) distinguishes release verification from actual account installation and behaviour.

Operational, broadcast and authored registers produce personal communications; documentation applies Andrew's quality standard in a neutral professional voice. Purpose and ownership decide the route, so a factual research report and an owned argument use different registers even when both are called reports. Factual synthesis can organise verified material and label implications without a prewritten personal thesis. Recommendations remain distinct from adopted decisions, and a covering email can have a different register from its attachment.

Natural-language invocation remains enabled for relevant execution requests. The description excludes exploration and the substance gate prevents invented positions. Source metadata does not prove actual client discovery or invocation. Current target examples illustrate the operative rules; retained source samples preserve useful evidence of voice without making every historical construction a current instruction.

The [plugin validation and repair](evidence/2026-09-voice-plugin-validation.md) makes representative capacity explicit and treats medium guidance as subordinate to purpose. Craft constraints apply to new prose while exact evidence is preserved; pack-wide checks do not grant authority over additional files. Constructed examples demonstrate the rules without becoming evidence of Andrew's authorship or endorsement. Contemporary endorsed finished pieces remain a calibration gap.

## Knowledge and continuity remain at the external boundary

The knowledge store holds notes and developing positions outside the kit. Portable operating files cannot rely on inaccessible vault notes or prescribe store schemas; that would couple behaviour to one implementation and invite invented connections. [ADR-005](decisions/adr-005-stewardship-is-behaviour.md) keeps stewardship judgement in the charter and store structure outside it.

Supplied files and project instructions are controlled context. Ambient memory and history are product-managed state whose enablement, retention and review belong in settings. The constitution governs deliberate tool-mediated changes without claiming to control automatic service retention.

Long-conversation continuity must preserve decisions, reasons, scope, approval limits and attribution. A holdings checkpoint marks each thread resolved, deliberately parked or awaiting input. Bare topic lists lose the state that makes the checkpoint useful. Recording a useful observation persistently still needs the applicable scoped approval.

## Shared derivations and direct delivery prevent forks

Independent condensed copies drift. Two reviewed sources therefore own the shared chat and execution wording; a distribution script copies marked bodies and source metadata into detached product files. Product wrappers remain manually maintained. [Contract maintenance](../kit/implementation/platforms/contract-maintenance.md) owns coverage and loading; exact copying does not establish semantic equivalence or behavioural adherence.

Shared bodies work alone, carry compact standards and defer to the full core when supplied. Cowork adds its separate safeguards to a declared base rather than becoming a third general contract. Skills and project context travel only when needed. The [deployment map](../kit/implementation/platforms/deployment-map.md) owns the product inventory, avoiding another inventory here.

Agents read accessible source files or receive the execution body and a task handover. Surfaces without repository access receive the required files or text directly; the kit does not automatically republish the personal constitution into secondary stores. The root `AGENTS.md` and `CLAUDE.md` govern this repository, not general deployment. No standing global deployment file is maintained; revisit that choice only if use demonstrates a need. Conductor is concurrency within the Code route, not a separate mode or adapter.

## Cowork retains additional delegation safeguards

Cowork reads mode from the request under the shared contract. Its separate plan-and-confirm requirement for file work is a deliberate delegation choice, not a rule to ask clarifying questions about an already sufficient brief. Confirmation already given for the exact plan and targets is reused.

The [Cowork addendum](../kit/implementation/platforms/claude/cowork/global-instructions.md) retains no overwrites, exact-file deletion approval, folder boundaries, connector previews, output defaults and approval for project-instruction changes. Broad cleanup wording does not identify deletions, and a drafting request does not approve sending. These choices do not rest on assumptions about product defaults. Simplifying documentation does not relax them or the voice documentation register's independent formal-deliverable review requirement.

## Skills track the working set

The [skills tracker](../kit/implementation/skills/README.md) records adopted capabilities, recurring purpose, upstream source and brief deployment status. Personal methodology stays version-controlled with the contract it extends. External packages remain with their maintainers, using managed delivery. Prefer each provider's useful native or published implementation in its own harness; independent skills may be shared where supported. This is an adopted configuration preference, not a comparative quality ranking.

A portable `SKILL.md` does not reproduce a whole plugin. Plugins may bundle tools, hooks and other components, while connectors supply access. Discovery, invocation, permissions and actual use require separate evidence.

Earlier separate capability/source registers were replaced by a provider-selection catalogue, then by the current working-set tracker. That progression reduced overlapping inventories and unused research without establishing comparative performance. Git preserves those abandoned structures. Reconsider an addition when recurring use gives it a job or reveals interference, rather than maintaining every offering that could be useful.

## Prompts retain distinctive interaction methods

Prompts are lightweight entry points under the constitution: purpose, situation and text to supply. Supporting files, procedural complexity or invocation machinery can justify a skill later. The experimental folder records evidence status, not a testing backlog or a commitment to promote.

The retained candidates serve particular participation mechanisms: reaction-based elicitation, learning an unfamiliar domain's evaluative standard, blind-spot questions, stress-testing a working position and checking ownership of delegated synthesis. The blind-spot prompt asks questions without teaching a finished frame first; the comprehension check remains optional and prompt-only. Generic analysis, research, critique and planning templates were retired because the operating contract already carries their useful standards. Discovery interviewing remains available for a project whose goals are unclear.

Reaction-based elicitation came from Thariq's “A Field Guide to Fable: Finding Your Unknowns”, adapted from its agentic-coding context. Coding-specific deviation logs, implementation notes, reference-code workflows, quiz-before-merge gates and the external unknowns taxonomy were not adopted as standing policy. External methods enter at the lowest sufficient layer and become permanent only when use justifies that decision.

## Model guidance and context cost stay in implementation

One constitution serves the working models; task needs determine whether a full core or condensed execution body is appropriate. Differences in brevity, progress reporting, effort controls or host composition do not justify different independence or approval policies. [Model guidance](../kit/implementation/platforms/model-guidance.md) owns dated observations and distinguishes vendor advice, owner choices and local evidence. Former July model routing remains historical in Git, not runtime voice craft.

Standing material consumes context when supplied. Keep bootstrap guidance focused, load conditional material only when relevant, and supply detailed craft at execution. Preserve substantive protections when condensing. Field lengths and host limits belong with adapters and need checking when deployed; fewer source bytes or catalogue entries do not establish token savings or better output.

## Structural decisions remain in ADRs

The [ADR index](decisions/README.md) preserves all twelve records and their statuses. ADR-001 and ADR-002 establish the five layers and maintenance-only philosophy. ADR-003 keeps framework and kit together while enforcing anonymisation. ADR-004 retains the default thinking-partner role inside the constitution: extraction would need a concrete independent consumer and a new structural decision, not architectural tidiness alone.

ADR-005 owns the store boundary; ADR-006 separates structural records from this behavioural rationale; ADR-007 places the evolution objective. ADR-008 owns naming. ADR-009 records the former deferral of physical alignment, ADR-010 selects semantic paths rather than numbered layer folders, and ADR-011 contains the personal instance under `kit/` alongside framework and governance. ADR-012 keeps the public repository self-contained without importing raw private working material. Repeating those decisions here would create another maintenance surface without preserving additional reasoning.

## Each maintenance record has one responsibility

The root overview orients readers. The framework remains an independently understandable generic pattern; current architecture describes this instance. Product guides explain setup, baselines record current saved-state observations and gaps, and contract maintenance owns derivation coverage. This register explains reasons; ADRs preserve structural decisions and supersession; dated evidence records methods, versions, results and limitations.

Historical evidence stays dated. Current source does not inherit an earlier deployment match or behavioural result. The July baseline remains evidence for its tested configuration, and an exclusion of evals for one named batch does not abolish the standing policy. A concise material record is useful; ordinary use and unused plans need not create more governance.

## Evaluations distinguish compliance, change and value

The [evaluation redesign](evidence/2026-09-evaluation-redesign.md) gives every scenario shared factual-grounding, ownership, authority and instruction/data checks. A narrow behavioural success cannot compensate for a breach elsewhere in the answer. Existing probes remain diagnostic examples; natural conversations and transfer cases broaden the evidence without pretending that synthetic dialogue measures Andrew's actual learning.

A previous-versus-proposed comparison tests a change. A no-compact-versus-compact comparison tests incremental value on the same host. Neither alone demonstrates independent judgement over time. The human review practice supplies that missing perspective without requiring a record after every conversation. Frozen plans, original grades, explicit adjudications and measured usage make the comparison inspectable. Tokens measure resource use alongside quality; brevity is not the purpose of the compact.
