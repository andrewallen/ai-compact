# July 2026 Evaluation Baseline

The dated evidence supporting the current behavioural guidance in AI Compact. It records what was evaluated, the outcomes adopted into the maintained files and the limits of the resulting claims.

## Status

**Current reference.** The five-layer architecture, three-domain repository structure, operating contract, platform adapters and active eval harness form one coherent system.

The knowledge-steward charter is an available role definition. Its presence does not assert that an autonomous steward is currently deployed.

## What was evaluated

The July comparison covered the constitution, condensed platform contracts and active fixtures for:

- agreement and sycophancy;
- option diversity and false balance;
- exploratory restraint and execution transitions;
- evolution without delegated cognition;
- separation between the model's register and Andrew's voice;
- deliberate persistence and ambient-memory boundaries;
- challenge and clarification thresholds;
- completion claims grounded in current-session evidence and exact approved targets.

GPT-5.6 completed the core fixture families and targeted controls. The available Claude Fable 5 comparison stopped after 17 calls because of the usage allowance, covering fixture families A–D and part of E/F. The evidence does not establish cross-provider parity.

## Adopted outcomes

- Ask for reviewable structure, rationale, evidence and trade-offs rather than private reasoning.
- Ask clarifying questions only where ambiguity would materially change the work.
- Preserve the challenge floor; targeted controls did not show systematic challenge over-triggering.
- Require tool-mediated completion claims to match current-session evidence and exact approved targets.
- Keep the assistant's thinking-partner register distinct from Andrew's output voice.
- Record long-conversation holdings as resolved, deliberately parked or awaiting input.
- Keep model-specific drafting guidance in the execution skill and durable behaviour in the constitution.

Targeted GPT-5.6 checks passed three runs out of three for material clarification, visible rationale, exact-target handling and voice separation D1–D3.

## Known limitations

The harness is diagnostic, not a certification:

- some sycophancy runs yielded too readily after an initially sound challenge;
- one option-generation run converged too closely on the conventional cluster;
- exploratory fixtures occasionally over-produced or omitted the expected return to the user;
- some outputs strengthened supplied facts into unsupported claims;
- ambient memory remains product-managed state outside the prompt layer's control;
- multi-turn fixtures simulated conversations inside fresh non-interactive runs.

These are claim boundaries and regression targets. They trigger changes only when observed behaviour or new evidence justifies a specific amendment.

## Evidence scope

The results above support the current operating contract and adapters; they do not certify every model, product version or future agent configuration. The active fixtures in `kit/evals/` are the reproducible source for subsequent comparisons.
