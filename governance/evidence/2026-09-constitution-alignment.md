## Independent review and resolved findings

An initial fresh-context review compared the old and rewritten constitution without the drafting conversation. It found four defects in the operating contract, all corrected:

1. **R1 — Exploratory analysis shortcut.** Clarified that calling an exploratory read “analysis” does not bypass the framing invitation; a defined analytical deliverable proceeds directly.
2. **R2 — Narrowed challenge pause.** Restored the pause for an unresolved material challenge that would weaken the result, rather than limiting it to assumptions only Andrew could resolve.
3. **R3 — Visibility ambiguity.** Removed “user-visible” as a qualifier over all persistent artefacts and explicitly included hidden supporting records.
4. **R4 — Exploratory detour signalling.** Restored the requirement to flag an assistant-introduced thread.

A second fresh-context, read-only review assessed the current files in its scope: the four constitution files, three standalone chat adapters, shared execution contract and four embedded copies, Cowork instructions and both templates, knowledge-steward charter, and the two changed voice-skill files. It compared them with the task-start snapshot where available and did not consult this record. It found one additional omission:

5. **R5 — Expansion in detached execution.** The condensed execution text required challenge but omitted the positive duty to extend reach and the active scrutiny floor. Both were added to the shared source and propagated to all four copies.

The second reviewer found no other actionable semantic losses within that scope. R5's final wording and propagation received a local static check; a further independent review of that repair was not run. Framework, prompt and governance alignment received local source review. These checks are not behavioural tests.
