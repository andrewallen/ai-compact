## Plan and authority

Andrew requested a point-in-time review of the constitution, followed by an interview one question at a time. After reviewing the resulting understanding, he requested a plan and implementation. This authorises the calibration and professional-overlay refresh and the necessary consistency and verification work. The interview and supplied meeting transcript remain outside the repository.

The implementation plan is:

1. Replace rolling experience counts with the October 1996 career anchor; add experimentation across AI providers and its contribution to learning and judgement in calibration.
2. Refresh the professional overlay around the confirmed ATS/CDTO role, commercial accountability, customer relationships, wider strategy and internal Microsoft coordination.
3. Keep useful account alignment and SPA24 grounding explicitly dated. Remove named colleagues and headcount; keep temporary initiatives and publication catalogues in task context.
4. Use an open set of professional perspectives, including organisational change and capability. Preserve ownership, evidence boundaries, neutral reasoning and conditional activation.
5. Check the framework lesson, platform derivations and affected fixtures; verify source integrity and run a bounded before/after evaluation where supported.

A temporary snapshot captured all 122 extant task-start files, including existing uncommitted work. The batch does not authorise committing, publication, live product deployment or changes to the external knowledge store.

## Implementation and rationale

Its professional-lens reference no longer prescribes a count. The professional overlay retains the same authority and activation declaration, while describing the confirmed role and commercial incentives, direct account responsibilities versus broader influence, and work to align Microsoft colleagues and partners.

The original six perspectives remain recognisable, with organisational change and capability explicit. Relevance determines which to apply. The fixed checklist and the blanket assumption about departmental sovereignty were removed. Customer needs, current decision ownership and the ability of both organisations to act now shape the application. The interview's broadly adopted AI/work narrative is recorded as a revisable working perspective, not an endorsement of every claim in the supplied talk.

Account alignment is dated 19 September 2026 and describes Andrew's territory rather than asserting an exhaustive government organisation chart. SPA24 is retained as one part of commercial grounding, with its original term and government-side owner. The November 2024 date is described as commencement rather than an independently verified signature date. Individuals and current initiative lists were not retained. The maintained reasoning is in [design decisions](../design-decisions.md#professional-context-separates-enduring-guidance-from-changing-facts); the generic lesson in the [layer model](../../framework/layer-model.md) is anonymised. No structural ADR is needed.

Calibration advances from 1.5 to 1.6; the overlay from 2.1 to 2.2, both dated 19 September 2026. Bootstrap and the operating contract remain unchanged from the task-start snapshot.
