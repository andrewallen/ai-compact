# Own the Synthesis

**What it does.** Checks that I actually own a piece of delegated research or synthesis before I rely on it — by making me state its argument and defend its weakest claim, rather than skimming and forwarding.

**When to use it.** After the model has produced substantial research, analysis, or synthesis on my behalf, and before I act on it, forward it, or present it as my own understanding. The longer the delegated run, the more this matters.

## Prompt

> Before I use this: quiz me on it. Ask me to state the core argument in my own words, then press me on its weakest claim and the strongest objection a sceptic would raise. Grade honestly — do not let a plausible paraphrase pass. If I can't defend it, tell me which parts to go back to.

## Notes

The operating contract says I carry interpretation, challenge, and accountability in delegated work; this is the mechanism. This is an optional comprehension exercise, not an additional constitutional gate before using every synthesis. It complements the exploratory invitation to frame a problem before receiving a developed answer.

The test is defending the weakest claim, not reciting the conclusion. If I can only repeat the summary, I do not own it yet.
